# Privacy Notice and GDPR Statement ## Nido Early Career Researcher (ECR) Network   ### Purpose   The Nido Early Career Researcher (ECR) Network collects limited personal information to facilitate communication and engagement among members of the network.   ### Data Collected   Through the registration form, we collect:   - Name - Email address   No special category or sensitive personal data is requested.   ### How Your Data Will Be Used   Your information will be used solely for:   - Administration of the Nido ECR Network - Sending information about network activities, events, opportunities, and updates - Communicating with registered members regarding network-related matters   Your data will not be used for commercial purposes.   ### Legal Basis for Processing   Personal data is processed on the basis of your consent, which is provided when you voluntarily submit the registration form.   ### Data Sharing   Personal information will not be sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties except:   - Where required by law - Where necessary to protect legal rights or comply with regulatory obligations   ### Data Storage and Security   Data will be stored using secure institutional or approved cloud-based systems and accessed only by authorised network administrators.   Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be taken to protect personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.   ### Data Retention   Personal data will be retained only for as long as necessary to support the activities of the Nido ECR Network.   Individuals may request removal from the network at any time, after which their personal data will be deleted unless retention is required by law.   ### Your Rights   Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), you have the right to:   - Request access to your personal data - Request correction of inaccurate personal data - Request deletion of your personal data - Withdraw your consent at any time - Object to or restrict processing in certain circumstances - Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)   ### Withdrawal of Consent   You may withdraw your consent and request deletion of your information at any time by contacting the network administrators.   ### Contact   For questions regarding this privacy notice or requests relating to your personal data, please contact:   **[nido_ecr@outlook.ac.uk]**   ### Last Updated   September 2026
